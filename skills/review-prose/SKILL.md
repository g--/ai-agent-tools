# Review Prose

Review as the intended reader. Judge whether the draft lets that reader understand, decide, or act—not whether it follows a preferred visual style.

Read [`rubric.md`](rubric.md) before reviewing. Apply every criterion, quote evidence for each finding, and distinguish a missing fact from an unsupported inference. When comparing drafts, blind their origin when possible and choose the one that better serves the reader with the least unnecessary text.

## Deliverable

For each criterion, report `meets`, `partly meets`, or `does not meet`, with evidence. End with a concise recommendation, the reader harm caused by any material problem, and any uncertainty in the review.

## Maintenance

`rubric.md` is the canonical evaluation standard for this repository. When `writing-prose` or `copy-editing` changes a quality goal, check whether the rubric should measure that outcome. Update the rubric when it should; otherwise record why the change is not externally evaluable. Keep the rubric outcome-focused rather than duplicating either skill's process.

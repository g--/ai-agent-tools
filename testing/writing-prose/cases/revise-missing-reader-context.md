# Case: revise a construction-closure announcement

## Test purpose

Test whether the skill restores the reader context needed to understand why a disruption is happening before describing its operational details.

## Intended artifact

A neighborhood announcement for residents near Riverside Park.

## Task

Revise the draft for its intended readers. Preserve supported facts and intended meaning. Do not introduce facts not present in the source material.

## Source material

The city is building the Riverside Park community pool, approved in last year's parks bond. Construction begins Monday, 6 May. The project replaces the disused wading pool and adds an accessible changing area, six-lane pool, and shaded seating. The construction work is expected to finish in August 2025.

From 6 May through 24 May, the east entrance to Riverside Park and the adjacent section of Willow Lane will close from 07:00 to 17:00 on weekdays so contractors can remove the old wading pool and bring excavation equipment into the site. Pedestrians can enter through the north entrance on Cedar Street. The playground, dog run, tennis courts, and west parking lot remain open. Residents who need accessible park access should use the Cedar Street entrance, where the accessible path remains available. Work may be noisy between 08:00 and 16:00.

The contractor is ParkBuild LLC. The excavation permit number is RP-2025-041. The project manager attended high school two blocks away. The construction fencing is blue, and the parks department expects to post weekly photographs on its internal project dashboard.

## Draft to revise

## East entrance and Willow Lane closures begin 6 May

The east entrance to Riverside Park and the adjacent section of Willow Lane will close from 07:00 to 17:00 on weekdays from 6 May through 24 May. Use the north entrance on Cedar Street instead.

The playground, dog run, tennis courts, and west parking lot remain open. The accessible path from Cedar Street remains available. Work may be noisy between 08:00 and 16:00.

## Evaluation criteria

The revision gives residents enough context to understand that the closures support the approved Riverside Park community-pool construction before asking them to navigate the disruption. It preserves dates, hours, alternate access, unaffected facilities, accessibility information, and noise timing. It omits contractor, permit, personal-history, fencing, and internal-dashboard details that do not help residents understand or respond to the announcement.

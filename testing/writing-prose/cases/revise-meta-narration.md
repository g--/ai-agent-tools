# Case: revise a scheduled-export announcement

## Intended artifact

A concise customer email announcing scheduled CSV exports to eligible workspace administrators.

## Task

Revise the draft for its intended readers. Preserve supported facts and intended meaning. Do not introduce facts not present in the source material.

## Source material

Starting 4 November, Business and Enterprise workspace administrators can schedule one saved report to export as CSV to an approved S3 bucket each day or week. Administrators need `Manage reports` permission and an allowlisted bucket. The first export runs in the next scheduled window rather than immediately. Free and Team workspaces are not eligible. The help article is `https://docs.example.test/scheduled-exports`.

## Draft to revise

Subject: Scheduled exports are now available

This email is for Business and Enterprise administrators who need to schedule CSV exports. This announcement explains how the feature works and how to configure it.

Administrators should open a saved report and select **Schedule export**. This section explains that they can choose daily or weekly delivery to an approved S3 bucket. You need `Manage reports` permission and an allowlisted bucket. The export does not run right away.

## Evaluation criteria

The revision orients eligible readers through feature, benefit, and action rather than narrating the email or its sections. It preserves eligibility, prerequisites, frequency, destination, and next-scheduled-window timing without adding claims.

# Decision Writing Supplementary Rubric

Use this with the shared `review-prose` rubric. Review each draft as both a current decision participant and a future engineer or manager investigating the record. Mark each criterion **meets**, **partly meets**, or **does not meet**, quoting evidence.

- **Decision-independent world state:** The world-state section stands alone and records contemporaneous facts, constraints, stakes, sources, and uncertainty without revealing the preferred or selected option.
- **Epistemic boundaries:** Facts, assumptions, forecasts, preferences, and judgments are distinguishable. The draft does not add hindsight or turn estimates into facts.
- **Complete decision path:** Criteria, viable options, evaluation, decision, rationale, and material consequences are connected in an understandable order.
- **Fair options and traceable rationale:** Viable alternatives receive enough concrete treatment to make the decisive trade-off auditable; the selected option follows from stated criteria and inputs.
- **Actionability and durability:** Owners, timing, follow-through, validation/review triggers, and material dependencies are present when supplied or needed.
- **Earned brevity and fit:** The draft excludes incidental implementation trivia and related background that does not change understanding or action, while retaining decision context unavailable from code or links.

Choose **A**, **B**, or **tie**, then explain which record would better support a sound current decision and later historical understanding.
